MERGED PROJECT BRIEF — Computer Lab Toolkit


Status: ACTIVE MERGED WORKSTREAM


Merged components
- Recovery Catalogue: reusable scenario packs containing symptoms, environment, diagnostics, repair steps, verification and evidence.
- Disk Sonar: storage-diagnostic orchestration and safe routing across identity/capacity/bridge/health checks.
- Driver Suite / Driver Indexer: driver inventory, provenance, backup/restore, searchable identity and official-source retrieval.
- QR Utility: retained only as a small utility capability, not a standalone project.


Supporting evidence
The separate 30_COMPUTER_LAB folder remains the device/evidence area for STGSivir, ASUS K72F, HP x360, storage/recovery and maintenance records.


Architecture rule
Do not build another generic disk utility or automatic driver updater. The differentiator is evidence-aware diagnostics: know the exact machine/drive, preserve prior tests, recommend the next safe diagnostic, and produce a recovery record.


Next proof
Take one real recovery case from intake -> identify machine/drive -> collect evidence -> route diagnostics -> propose/perform a bounded repair -> verify -> emit a reusable Recovery Catalogue scenario.
